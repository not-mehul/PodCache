Removing the ads

Algorithm. Volume drops and jingles are unreliable, so PodCache never guesses at the audio. It transcribes every sentence with Whisper, asks a language model which sentences are sponsor reads, and cuts on those timestamps.

Engine. faster-whisper transcribes; a language model finds the ads; ffmpeg stream-copies and stitches; mutagen restores the cover art and tags. Privacy. The server binds to 127.0.0.1 and downloads live in a local folder.
